    HOW TO PLAY

    Left thumb anywhere on the left half: move. Your weapon auto-aims the nearest monster.

    The two round buttons (right) are your hero's abilities. The Wanderer has DASH + NOVA; the Pyromancer, Ranger and Bone Knight each bring their own pair and a passive. Recruit and pick heroes at the Hero Hall.

    Elites (glowing) and bosses drop gear for 9 slots: weapon, helmet, amulet, chest, gloves, ring, belt, pants, boots. Walk over loot to drop it in your BAG (10 slots) without stopping. Tap BAG any time: time freezes while you compare (▲/▼ POWER), equip into the matching slot, or JUNK it (junk is deleted, it never lands on the floor). Loot lying near you shows under ON THE GROUND: inspect, EQUIP, TAKE or JUNK it without bagging it first. JUNK ▼ clears every bag item that's worse than what you wear. The LOOT filter (Settings only) hides Commons, or everything below Rare, so they never drop; boss and mini-boss loot always drops. It applies to ground and bag. Magic and Rare items roll affixes; armour rolls smaller, slot-specific ones.

    1.9.5 RAID REMATCHES: every raid boss remembers how often you beat it. Clear it N times and the next fight is rematch tier N: more boss HP, harder hits, slightly faster casts and telegraphs, but more gold and XP, more and better Rares, a bigger Unique chance and a better Quartermaster fill. Steps are big for the first 5 clears, then shrink (soft cap around tier 10). The first kill is still the trophy.

    1.12.0 COMBAT CEILING: late waves punish standing still. Poison now stacks up to 24 (it still never kills on its own, and DASH sheds a quarter of the pile). Statuses stack too (up to x5, the number shows on the tile): each bolt or pulse that hits again makes SLOWED / WEAKENED / FRAGILE worse, standing in a hex cloud adds a stack every 2 s, and NO REGEN at x2+ slowly rots your HP. Hex Orbs and hex casters fire fans of status bolts late (every bolt shows its coloured aim line). Some foes grow a gold shell after a flashing ring tell: it is immune for 2 s and charges you, so kite it until the shell breaks (then it takes +25% for a moment).

    1.11.1 SUPPORT + PREMIUM: there are no ads between runs any more. Tap the ♥ button on the hub (next to HOW TO) to support the creator: watch an ad (a thank-you + a ♥ on your hub, as often as you like) or buy PREMIUM once. Premium doubles your stash (12 / 24 / 40 → 24 / 48 / 80), adds an endless ★ UNIQUES tab (every Unique you extract goes there, never counts against the stash and is never salvaged, so you can collect them all), and makes the once-per-run revive (and, from 1.13, the death-screen save one) free. When you go down after your Hearth lives, the game asks once per run: revive instead of dying right now?

    1.13.0 LOADOUT + STASH (no insurance): gear you find is only safe once you extract (exit gate or a won raid): then everything you carry is banked. Pieces you took from your loadout go back into the loadout; the rest goes to your STASH (12 slots, 24 with the Armory, 40 with the Artifact Museum; when it is full the weakest pieces are salvaged for gold). Between runs, open ▣ STASH and pick your LOADOUT: the stash pieces you take into the next run (3 slots, 6 with the Armory, 9 with the Museum). They are equipped at START, free. Die or abandon before extracting and everything you carried is lost, loadout pieces too; the stash is never touched. On the death screen you may watch one ad to save ONE lost piece back to your stash (Premium: free).

    1.10.0 POISON: Blight Adders (bite), Venom Spitters (violet globs) and Blightcaps stack poison on you: a violet tile with a number shows your stacks. It ticks, then wears off on its own (it never kills). Your quick ability (DASH / METEOR / ROLL / CHARGE) sheds 2 stacks. Blightcaps burst when they die: their violet ring shows the blast, so kill them at range or step out while the corpse swells. Shield Phalanxes block hits from the front (circle them); Rubble Hurlers lob rocks at a shadow on the ground. New gear: Moon Chakram, Blight Darts, 8 armour bases and 6 new affixes. Raid bosses 6-8: Vexmire, Kharos, Skarn.

    1.9.6 HEX CLOUDS + BOLTS: cursed ground is now a cloud: stay out of its bright edge. Some small clouds (an arrow next to their tag) drift slowly after you - just sidestep, they turn slowly. Hex Orbs and Hex Casters (a status glyph over their head) also fire slow status bolts: watch the coloured aim line, it blinks when the aim locks, then step sideways. A bolt hit = that status for 5 s. NO REGEN blocks every heal, life steal included.

    1.9.4 HEXES: from wave 4 a slow Hex Orb drifts in. When it stops and draws a ring, step out (or dash) before it pulses, or it curses you for 7 s. Cursed ground works the same way: a marked corner or ring warns for 2.5 s, then hexes you while you stand in it. Statuses (dark tiles next to your buffs): SLOWED move -35% · NO REGEN no healing at all · WEAKENED damage dealt -30% · FRAGILE damage taken +35%.

    1.9.3 PRICES: the first village buys are cheap again (Smithy 1,500, Hero Hall 2,000, Tavern L2 1,200, first MEND 1,000) and prices climb in steps: early buildings ~5-12k, mid tiers tens of thousands, the Portal and end prestige still cost hundreds of thousands to millions. Heroes cost 7,500 / 10,000 / 12,500 to recruit, and a portal drop costs about 1,000-2,000 gold.

    1.9.2 SCREEN FIT: menus and cards now fit (or scroll) on short phone screens, the run HUD and buttons stay clear of the camera cut-out, rounded corners and the gesture bar, and the pixels are scaled in whole device pixels so they stay sharp on every screen.

    1.9.1 COMPACT HUD: the run screen shows HP + lives, level, your weapon, gear pips (gold dot = ★ kept), WAVE, time / kills, the event line and danger lines. Buffs are small coloured tiles (gold frame = for the rest of the run, the bar under a tile is its time left). Gold, gear count, the next boss / gear check and buff names + times are on the pause card. Your held weapon now points where you aim, and dropped hearts are bigger with a pink glow (they pulse softly before they fade).

    1.9 SETTINGS + PREMIUM: ⚙ (hub) or ⚙ SETTINGS (pause) holds sound ♪, music ♫, banners, PREMIUM and RESET PROGRESS. (1.11.1: no more ads between runs.) Ads are only ever optional: when you go down, watch an ad to revive (once per run), or tap the hub ♥ (Support the creator). PREMIUM is a one-time purchase; the revive then happens free. Nothing that gives power is for sale.

    1.8 SOUND: ♪ on the hub mutes everything (sound effects and music). ♫ (hub) or MUSIC (pause menu) turns only the music off. Each map has its own music (camp, Ashen Field, Bone Crypt, Frost Spire, raids) that keeps changing and picks up when the horde or a boss shows up. Loot sounds tell rarity apart: Rares chime, Uniques ring out. All sound is generated on the phone, no downloads.

    1.7 EXTRACT OR LOSE IT: gear only survives a run if you extract. An EXIT GATE rises every 10 waves (10, 20, 30...) but it is SEALED: beat its trial first (kill monsters inside its red ring, stand on its 3 runes in order, slay the gate KEEPER, or on a Gear Check wave beat the Warden), then stand in it 5s. Extracting pays a bonus and opens the KEEP picker (tap an item for its full details). (1.13.0: death loses everything you carry, loadout too; see LOADOUT + STASH above.) A won raid also counts as an extraction. Barracks and catapults are now far outposts that grow if you ignore them (hut, war camp, citadel; catapult, siege squad, artillery mage), and an ignored battery's shells hit harder every second. Village costs went up x1000, XP is halved and there is one skill-point pool for the shared and hero trees. Some uniques now form sets (2-3 piece bonuses). The Cleaver hits a frontal cone. Raids hit much harder: dodge the telegraphs, and punish the boss while it is OPEN after each cast.

    Gems are character XP. Level ups give skill points that you keep forever. Weapons are lost when the run ends.

    Waves never stop. Every 5th wave brings a boss event. Between waves watch for a regen well (stand in it to heal and fill the meter) and the gold goblin (it runs away: catch it before it escapes off-screen). Win an event for a 60–90s power-up: multi-shot, slow aura, lifesteal or freeze burst.

    0.8 events (follow the lettered edge arrows): E escort: guard the mage until her teleport finishes, or walk the caravan to camp (it only rolls while you stay close). Raiders wear a red mark. B barracks keep sending packs and a C catapult lobs shells at red circles until you smash them. G exit gate: stand in it 6s to escape with a gold + XP bonus and a rare gift. Dying still ends the run normally.

    0.9 tester pack: on waves 20, 40, 60 a GEAR CHECK warden replaces the Colossus: kill it before its CULL timer (40s) runs out or it enrages. Red ring = slam (step out), red line = dash (step sideways), red glow = bullet rings. Passing it drops 2 Rares and a RUN boon (a power-up that lasts the whole run; wave-10+ Colossus kills give one too). Loot keeps improving past wave 20 (T4 Exalted) and 40 (T5 Mythic). Barracks are armoured and catapults fire from much farther: smash them early. The exit gate shows its escape bonus above the arch. Build the PORTAL CIRCLE in the village to start a run at wave 21 / 31 / 41 (later tiers: 61 / 81 / 101, each needs that Gear Check passed): tap ⊙ next to START to pick the wave; each use costs gold.

    1.0 Market: from wave 6 a travelling M market stall sets up near you (every ~2.5 min, open 50s). Walk into it: time freezes while you buy 6 items rolled for this wave (Magic+, one Rare) or sell bag and worn items for gold (a sale pays ~42% of what the stall would ask). It spends your gold bank. Your weapon in hand and ★ kept Armory items can't be sold. LEAVE resumes the fight with a short grace; the stall packs up 12s later (walk back in to trade again). RESET PROGRESS (next to the version on the start screen) wipes the whole save after you type WIPE; SKILLS → RESPEC only refunds shared points.

    1.1 content: the shared tree has 15 nodes (Bloodlust, Last Gasp, Splinter, Catalyst, Momentum, Midas, Packrat are new) and every hero tree has 9 (a third row under each ability, plus a second passive node). New foes: the Cinder Mason raises stone walls across your path (dashed orange line first; walls only block you, last 5.5s, and Dash / Roll / Charge pass through), Blast Ticks arm and explode (red circle: back off or kill them first), Corpse Bloats burst into maggots, Wraiths mark a purple X next to you and blink there, Rime Oracles heal and shield their pack (kill them first). From wave 10 the Colossus, and later big elites and mini-bosses, drop big red circles on you: get out before they fill. New weapons: Rift Spear (line thrust), Bolt Thrower (3-bolt burst, boss killer), Chain Flail (360° spin). New affixes: Slayer's, Execution, Thunder, Fleet, Greed, Thorns. Orange Uniques are named pieces with a special effect (Wardens drop them most).

    1.2 village: your town now grows as you build. The village score upgrades the roads (dirt path, gravel, cobbles, dressed stone) and the homes (thatch shacks, tiled cottages, two-storey townhouses), brings in more trees, flowers, folk and animals, and a windmill once the town is big enough. Purely cosmetic: buildings work exactly as before.

    Know your foes: swarms come in packs; tanks are big and shrug off knockback; chargers stop, flash and draw a red dash line before they rush (step sideways); casters keep their distance and show an aim line before they fire; a mini-boss (orange ring) shows up on waves 3, 8, 13... and the Bone Colossus every 5th wave. Each map has its own cast and hazards: Ashen Field ember vents glow then erupt (they burn monsters too), Bone Crypt graves crack open and raise skeletons, Frost Spire ice sheets slow you down. Zones get meaner: Bone Crypt is zone threat II and Frost Spire zone threat III (monsters scale as if several waves deeper, hit harder and come thicker; more gold and XP). Ashen Field stays the base curve. Gear Checks past wave 60 (Void Warden IV, V...) dash twice.

    Gold (coins) is kept forever. Spend it in the Village: the tavern brews gold while you're away, the hearth buys extra lives and wave healing, the smithy picks your starting weapon. New buildings and tiers need gold AND a milestone (the card shows ✓/✗ for each): Hero Hall, Waygate (Bone Crypt, then Frost Spire at tier 2), Inn / Stone Hearth / Forge tiers that make the tavern, hearth and smithy stronger, and prestige decor that fills the town (walls, statue, garden, banners, lanterns, fountain, market, watchtower, trophy wall). Drag the village to look around; a ! sign means you can build it now. The ARMORY (and then the Artifact Museum) grows your loadout slots (3 / 6 / 9) and your stash. Loadout pieces are equipped at the next run start; the smithy weapon fills the weapon slot if your loadout has none (or rides along as a spare).

    1.4 raids: after your first boss kill a 🏆 RAIDS button appears next to VILLAGE. The Raid Board lists 5 staged bosses (Ashmaw, Morthal, Glacia, Vael, Morrghast), each with 3 phases; each needs the previous trophy and a progress milestone (the card shows ✓/✗). CHALLENGE drops you into a sealed arena with your Armory kit plus raid-issued Magic gear in empty slots. Read the floor: orange / red circles = get out, green = stand in before the countdown ends, cyan rings = slip through the GAP, purple lines = step off, an eye that follows you = keep moving, and some phases shield the boss until you kill the marked adds. The first kill mounts a trophy on the Trophy Wall and drops 2 Rares; then walk into the gold ring to return. Dying just ends the attempt.

    BANNER (hub, next to ♪, or the pause menu) sets the big announcements (WAVE, events, bosses, Market…): NORMAL large, SMALL smaller and see-through, TOP compact, or OFF. Since 1.11.2 every mode sits in the top band under the wave timer and the objective lines, never over your hero. The HUD lines under the timer and the edge arrows always stay.
